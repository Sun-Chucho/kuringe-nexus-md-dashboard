Download as Desktop App

Install Kuringe Nexus directly onto your Windows Desktop with the official KU logo shortcut.

  1. Look at the right side of your Chrome address bar (top right).
  2. Click the Install App icon (⤓ Install) or Chrome menu (⋮).
  3. Select "Install Kuringe Nexus | MD Dashboard" (or Save and share → Install...).
  4. Click Install to place the app logo directly on your Desktop!
